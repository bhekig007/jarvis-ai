Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Jarvis AI is a content planning and publishing assistant operated by bhekig007. The app helps create, review, and manage short-form content for connected accounts.

Information We Process

Jarvis may process content drafts, publishing preferences, connected account identifiers, and authorization tokens needed to connect to supported platforms such as X. The current static version stores planned posts in your browser local storage.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to generate drafts, prepare posting plans, and, when connected through an authorized API flow, publish or manage content you approve.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with platform APIs only when required to provide the requested functionality.

Security

Do not share passwords with Jarvis AI. Platform access should be granted only through official OAuth authorization flows and can be revoked from the connected platform settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository: github.com/bhekig007/jarvis-ai.
